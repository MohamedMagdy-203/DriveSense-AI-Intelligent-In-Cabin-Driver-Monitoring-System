# EAR Open-Eyes Frame Calibration Results

The camera ran for 30 seconds while the eyes remained open naturally with
normal blinking. EAR was recorded for every frame where a face was detected.
MediaPipe Face Landmarker ran on CPU using GStreamer camera input.

- Valid EAR frames: `883`
- Mean EAR: `0.3097`
- Standard deviation: `0.0646`
- Minimum EAR: `0.0825`
- Maximum EAR: `0.4912`
- 5th percentile: `0.2043`
- 95th percentile: `0.4391`
